# Statement of Work: Diminishing Musharaka Implementation for Gulf Credit Corporation [SAMPLE]
**Document Reference: SOW-2022-GCC-044 [SAMPLE]**  
**Customer: Gulf Credit Corporation**  
**Contact: Tariq Mansoor (tariq.mansoor@gulfcredit.com, +966-11-444-2211)**

## 1. Project Background & Scope
Gulf Credit Corporation requires the configuration of AzentioFinance to support Sharia-compliant auto leasing and residential home financing under the Diminishing Musharaka (Musharaka Mutanaqisa) structure in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Scope includes:
- Deployment of Diminishing Musharaka partnership share valuation engine.
- Monthly rental recalculation module synchronized with SAMA regulatory disclosure requirements.
- Integration with Sadad payment gateway and Yakeen identity verification services.

## 2. Professional Services & Commercial Terms [CONFIDENTIAL]
Gulf Credit agreed to pay SAR 3,800,000 for full system license and on-premise installation services, along with an ongoing support contract of SAR 450,000 annually.
Hourly rate for additional technical consulting was established at $150/hr.
