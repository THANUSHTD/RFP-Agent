# Past RFP Winning Response: Al-Hilal Bank Islamic Core Lending [SAMPLE]

**Bidder: Azentio Software**

## Question 3.1: Sharia Compliance and AAOIFI Certification
**Question**: Provide details on how your solution guarantees compliance with AAOIFI Shariah standards, particularly for Murabaha and Ijarah contracts.  
**Response (Azentio)**: AzentioFinance v3.0 has received explicit Sharia fatwas and verification confirming full adherence to AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 8 (Murabaha to the Purchase Orderer) and Standard No. 9 (Ijarah). In our Murabaha module, the software strictly prevents the generation of the client sale offer until positive confirmation of the commodity purchase and title receipt from the supplier is registered in the audit ledger. Furthermore, late payment fees are never added to the bank's profit ledger; the platform automatically transfers the donation portion to an isolated charity disbursement escrow.

## Question 4.2: Disaster Recovery, SLAs, and System Availability
**Question**: State your High Availability (HA) architecture and Disaster Recovery (DR) metrics including RTO and RPO.  
**Response (Azentio)**: AzentioFinance v3.0 offers a 99.95% production availability SLA when deployed across dual active-passive availability zones. Our automated geo-replication guarantees a Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 15 minutes or less, and a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of under 2 hours. Automated DR failover drills can be executed without data loss.
